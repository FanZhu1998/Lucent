# Performance baseline, 2026-10-10 (W13 closure)

The second measurement of Lucent's performance ([ADR 0021](../adr/0021-performance-baseline.md),
closure amendment of W13), made because W13 corrects the lenses of exactly matched photos by
default ([ADR 0023](../adr/0023-lens-profile-corrections.md)): measured against the
[first baseline](baseline-2026-10-09.md), four of its budgets fail for that reason (below), so the
budgets in [`budgets.json`](budgets.json) are now derived from this run. Every figure below comes
from the JSON in [`data/2026-10-10/`](data/2026-10-10/), rendered by
`tools\bench\Format-BenchReport.ps1`. The blueprint's figures are engineering targets, not claims,
and nothing here turns them into one.

- **Build:** `release` preset of branch `feat/w13-lensfun` (commit d728f5f plus the W13 closure
  working tree: the lens corrections' closing review, including the edit session's kept
  lens-corrected proxy), MSVC 19.44, triplet x64-windows-lucent, the bundled lens database 1.0.0
  and camera support database 2.1.0. Start-up runs used the installed layout
  `tools\package.ps1 -StageOnly` staged (391 files, 72.4 MiB).
- **Harness:** `tools\bench\Invoke-LucentBench.ps1`, all ten scenarios, three repetitions after a
  discarded warm-up each (ADR 0021, decision 9), 51 min, 21:33 to 22:24 UTC. The machine was
  otherwise idle (2.4 % CPU before the run, 3.35 GiB of memory available), unlike the first
  baseline's loaded machine, so most figures are lower than then for that reason alone.
- **One scenario measured again.** In the full run every 100 % view was 0.25 to 0.68 s slower than
  when the scenario was repeated, the Canon R5's included, which no lens correction touches: 2.15 s,
  against 1.36 to 1.81 s (median 1.39 s) in the 24 runs that followed within ten minutes (this
  build in the staged layout and in the build tree, this build without the lens database, and the
  W13 build before the closure). The preview scenario ran right after the cold starts, whose fresh
  copies of the installed layout the antivirus scans. `preview` was measured again at 22:31 UTC
  with the machine idle (`machine-preview.json`, 2.9 % CPU before), and that measurement is the
  baseline's; the full run's preview figures were: R5 2.15 s, ILCE-7M4 2.07 s, DJI FC4382 1.06 s,
  X-T4 3.13 s, Z 6 2.06 s.
- **Reading the figures:** "median [min-max] (CV)" over the three recorded repetitions; with three
  values the nearest-rank p95 is the maximum. Per-image figures first take each image's median.
- **Not repeated:** the 1,000-file batch resilience gate (ADR 0021, decision 6) passed on
  2026-10-09; W13 changes the worker protocol's result message (version 3), which the batch tests
  cover, not the batch machinery the gate exercises.

## What the lens corrections cost

The fixtures whose camera and lens the lens database matches exactly are now corrected by default
(ADR 0023, section 11: the Nikon Z 6, the Sony ILCE-7M4 and ILCE-7RM5, the Fujifilm GFX 50S II and
X-T4, the Leica M10 and the Canon 5D Mark III DNG; of the smoke tier's five, the Z 6, the ILCE-7M4
and the X-T4). Against the 2026-10-09 budgets this run fails four
(`Test-BenchBudgets.ps1 -DataDir docs\performance\data\2026-10-10` with the first baseline's
`budgets.json`), each the full-resolution correction's own cost:

| Budget | 2026-10-09 baseline | Budget | This run | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `preview.z6.view_100` | 1,144 ms | 1,430 ms | 1,615 ms | the 100 % view resamples the whole 24.5 MP image once (distortion, lateral CA, vignetting) |
| `preview.a7iv.view_100` | 1,000 ms | 1,250 ms | 1,550 ms | the same for 32.9 MP (distortion, lateral CA) |
| `batch.smoke-jpeg.workers_together` | 941 MiB | 1,040 MiB | 1,176 MiB | a worker whose export resamples keeps the corrected image beside stage one's during the first pass (ADR 0023, Consequences: about 0.5 GiB for 45 MP) |
| `batch.all-jpeg.workers_together` | 1,557 MiB | 1,720 MiB | 1,888 MiB | the same, two workers together |

The interactive figures the W13 build failed before its closure (the Z 6's drags: slider to frame
165 to 171 ms, develop service 139 to 148 ms) are back within the first baseline's budgets: the
edit session corrects its proxy once and reuses it (ADR 0007 decision 16): the Z 6's exposure drag
79 ms, its develop service 69 ms, the shadows drag 100 ms. Exports of the corrected photos take
longer in proportion to their size (the Z 6's JPEG 3.04 s instead of 2.58 s, the ILCE-7RM5's 7.02 s
instead of 6.30 s), within their budgets, and the export median over all images is unchanged
(2.60 s against 2.58 s).

## Budgets

`budgets.json` is derived from this run (`Test-BenchBudgets.ps1 -Create -BaselineDir
docs\performance\data\2026-10-10`) with the rules of ADR 0021, decision 5, unchanged: 79
candidates, 68 budgets, 11 tracked. Against the first baseline's candidates, `startup.warm.peak_working_set`
and `edit.z6.exposure_step` are budgets now (their variation fell on the idle machine), and
`edit.r5.exposure_drag_max` (CV 15 %) and `auto_cancel.final_check` (54 %) are tracked. The
budgets of the idle-machine baseline that ADR 0021's decision 9 plans for the W12 licence and
release phase (with the over-images statistics gated on their own repetition variability) replace
these.
